UDRP Research and Practical Guides

Wei Bin | LONG AN LAW FIRM | September 2026

This hub collects the English-language UDRP material published on this site. It is written for domain holders, brand owners and cross-border counsel who need to organise a China-related UDRP file. Each item links to the full page; the citations in those pages are to public decisions, and the lawyer did not act in any cited case.

The Chinese UDRP hub at /udrp/ covers the same subject in more depth. It includes material that has not been translated into English, so the Chinese and English pages are not page-for-page equivalents.
